Bench100 Privacy Policy

Bench100 collects nothing. Your records and videos stay on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no servers of ours.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Information we collect

None. The app uses no analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, or data-collecting third-party SDKs. The developer never receives your weights, reps, body weight, videos, or device identifiers.

Where your data is stored

Workout records (weights, reps, dates), body weight, and settings are stored inside the app on your device. They are included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which only you control. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Camera, microphone, and Photos

When you record a video to check your form or to remember an attempt, the app uses the camera and microphone. Videos are saved inside the app, and to the Photos app only if you choose to. The app only adds to Photos; it does not read your existing photos or videos (except a video you pick to import). Videos never leave your device.

Notifications

Rest-timer notifications are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server or token, so nothing about notifications leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

Bench100 Plus (monthly, yearly, or one-time) is processed by Apple through the App Store. The app only asks Apple whether your Apple ID owns Plus. Payment details are handled by Apple and are never visible to the app or the developer. You can manage or cancel auto-renewing subscriptions at any time in iPhone Settings.

Health data

The app does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not medical or coaching advice

Bench100 suggests weights and reps for training. It does not diagnose or give treatment advice. If you have a medical condition or pain, consult a doctor before starting.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: nosunosukawa@gmail.com
