The app that decides your next bench press weight, all the way to 100 kg.

Bench100 uses the 5×5 rules to set your next weight. What to do when you stall, when you take a break, and when you attempt 100 kg is decided from the start.

Everything you need to reach 100 kg (225 lb) with 5×5 is free: every session's weight, the guide, and stall help. Exporting your records (CSV) is free too.

Bench100 Plus (paid) builds add-on exercises (accessories, paused bench, stall-breakers) into your daily workout. From 1.2, Plus also includes 3 weeks of peaking before an attempt, 4-week waves beyond your goal, the projection to your goal, and keeping videos in the app. If you've used Bench100 since 1.1 or earlier, the projection and in-app videos stay free for you. Recording and saving videos to Photos is free for everyone.
